merge: a green holding a copy is checkpointed -- version 4 carries the copies' records, the copied trees' keys and the identity's sum (A-626 part 3, before its Studio run)

Green::checkpoint answered None at or after any copy (copied_by), and the service kept such a green's chain whole
(merge.checkpoint.unavailable). Version 4 of the checkpoint carries what a restore from the origin and the changed
keys could not rebuild: every copy record made at or before the version (destination, version, source, source
version); each copied tree's keys holding then what the copy above them gave them, read at the version as every
reader reads it, which the restore sets with no history so each reads by its record again (left out of the changed
tables); and the identity's running sum at the version (LtHash16's 2,048 bytes), moved there from the head as a
branch moves it (identity_moved_to), which the restore takes as the green's, keeping what it holds beyond the
restored state's entries (copies_before) so the recount still equals the identity. A version 3 checkpoint is refused
BadVersion; a key or a copy record written twice is refused DuplicatePath. Golden vectors derived by an independent
BLAKE3 written in Python from the BLAKE3 paper (2,497 bytes, 13b881c8...29251df3), which reproduces the empty input,
the official vectors at 1 to 3,072 bytes and A-646's ops-document vector; the copy oracle restores every history's
green from its checkpoint at every version and replays the rest.
