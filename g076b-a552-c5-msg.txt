server: a taken-back writable copy's claim is owed for the failover delay its shard holds, the term its holder's write lease runs for, not the client's old two-second reconnect budget (A-552, D-c 5 of 5)

A copy a successor took back from its anchor (A-197, Linux) waits for its holder to name it in a sync or a release;
one unclaimed within the client's reconnect budget (the recovery budget and a window, mirrored from
`slates_client::Deadlines`), counted from the shard's start, was written back and let go with its attachment. No
client keeps that budget any more: since A-468 a client reconnects while its anchor lives, by no clock. The map keeps
its holder's write attachment, and with it the holder's write lease (D-16), which a silent holder keeps for the failover
delay since A-414. So the claim is now owed for the failover delay the shard holds (`direct_map::claim_overdue`,
cfg-free): the shard records when it took the map back (`held_copy::taken_back_ns`, replacing `claim_deadline`), and each
reclaim judges that instant by the delay the shard holds then (A-363's estimate, fanned to every shard).
`reconnect_budget_ns` is deleted. D-c is closed by this and the four commits before it.
