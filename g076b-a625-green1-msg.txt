http-edge: the HTTP edge's engine -- one connection's request loop as a state machine over bytes: HTTPS through rustls (TLS 1.3 and 1.2, ALPN http/1.1, no client certificate, no resumption), Basic and Bearer credentials, 100-continue, keep-alive, bodies streamed as slices or inflated runs, typed refusals and the staged close; http: reply::bare (A-625)

- connection: `next()` asks for what the connection needs (write first, then read, decide, a body run, the body's end,
  the reply, a staged close's half-close and drain, or close); the reply's head is rendered at the decision (fields a
  reply cannot carry refuse 500) and sent with its first bytes, chunked to HTTP/1.1 and framed by the close to HTTP/1.0;
  100 Continue to a client waiting for it alone; a refusal leaving no body unread keeps the connection, any other ends
  in stages; a service failing before its reply is refused, after it aborts short of the last chunk; a TLS failure ends
  the connection and nothing reopens it.
- tls: ServingIdentity (the operator's chain and key, checked when made) builds one rustls configuration per
  connection (D-8 exception 2, one owner); HttpsSession reads, seals in the pieces rustls takes, and closes.
- credential: strict base64, Basic refused in cleartext, b64token, no control byte in a user or password (RFC 7617
  §2), the challenges with a quoted realm, secrets compared to their end.
- slates-http: `reply::bare`, a head with no caller fields that is never refused, so no refusal swallows an error.
